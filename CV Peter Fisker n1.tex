\section{Research Profile}
(261 citations since 2020, H-index: 9. \href{https://scholar.google.dk/citations?user=VpEv4Q4AAAAJ&hl=en}{Link to Google Scholar})\vspace{0.2cm}\\
\textbf{Geo-spatial Prediction Models of Poverty and the impacts of Climate Change}

\noindent My research revolves around the combination of economic and geographic variables. In particular, I aim to advance the use of remote sensing data within economics through improved measurements of weather, climate, disasters, poverty, and infrastructure. Most of my work is focused on the African continent, where I develop new methods for measuring poverty by combining satellite data and household level data. Extensive network and experience in Mozambique, Ethiopia, Kenya and Tanzania.
